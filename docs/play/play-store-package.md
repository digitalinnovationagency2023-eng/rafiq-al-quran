## 1. Privacy Policy (public page copy)

**Last updated: 20 October 2026**

Rafiq Al-Quran is published by Digital Innovative Agency FZ LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with data.

**What stays on your device.** Your questions, the answers shown, bookmarks, reading history, saved notes and preferences (language, text size, voice setting) are stored locally on your device. No account is required to use the app.

**What may leave your device.** The core experience — reading the Quran, searching, and receiving answers — works entirely offline using a dataset bundled inside the app. Data leaves the device only in these cases:
- **Feedback reports**, when you choose to send one. Only the category, your description, the interface language, the optional verse reference and optional contact detail are sent. Conversation text and diagnostics are included only if you explicitly tick the consent box.
- **Optional online services** (for example purchase verification if paid tools are enabled) when the app is configured with an API address.

**Voice features.** Speech input uses your device's speech recognition, which may be processed by your device platform provider. Spoken answers use your device's text-to-speech. The app does not record or upload audio. Voice features can be disabled in the app menu.

**Payments.** If optional premium tools are enabled, payments are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card details. Purchase tokens are sent to our server only to confirm entitlement.

**Donations.** Voluntary donations are processed by Google Play and are never required to read or search the Quran.

**What we never do.** We do not sell data, we do not run advertising, we do not use analytics identifiers, and the app never reads RQM Books accounting, customer, invoice or payroll data.

**Children.** The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

**Retention and deletion.** Locally stored data is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Feedback reports are kept for review and can be deleted on request. In-app conversations can be deleted with "New conversation".

**Your choices.** Use the app's Privacy page to review this information, disable voice, and delete conversations. Contact info@rqmbooks.com for data requests.

**Changes.** We will update this page when the app changes; the date above shows the latest revision.

## 2. Terms of Use (public page copy)

1. **Free core experience.** Reading, searching and the conversational verse-finding experience are free. Optional premium tools are conveniences and never change Quranic text, its sources, or the accuracy of an answer.
2. **Nature of the service.** The app is an AI-assisted search tool for Quranic verses and identified translations. It is **not** a religious authority, does not issue fatwas or rulings, and does not provide authoritative interpretation. Always consult qualified scholars where interpretation is required.
3. **Sources.** Quranic text and translations are served from the dataset bundled with the app; each passage shows its source, version and checksum. Translation licence status is shown explicitly.
4. **Acceptable use.** Do not use the app to misrepresent its output as divine communication or as a religious ruling.
5. **Donations.** Donations are voluntary, are not a purchase, and confer no additional religious authority or access to Quranic content.
6. **Premium tools.** If enabled, billing, renewal and cancellation are handled by Google Play under its terms. Prices and renewal periods are shown before purchase.
7. **Liability.** The app is provided "as is". To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for decisions made in reliance on search results.

## 4. Store listing copy

**App name:** Rafiq Al-Quran: AI Assistant

**Short description (≤80 chars):** Find Quran verses with a respectful, source-transparent AI assistant.

**Full description:**
Rafiq Al-Quran is a free, Quran-focused assistant that helps you find and read verses respectfully. Ask a question in plain language and the app retrieves relevant passages from the Quran, showing the Arabic text, an identified translation, the surah and verse reference, and the exact source and version of the dataset.

Key features
- Quran-only answers: no Hadith, Tafsir or external commentary, and never invented verses.
- Complete Quran: all 114 surahs with Arabic text and translations in ten languages.
- Honest answers: when the search cannot find sufficiently relevant verses, the app says so instead of guessing.
- Complete transparency: every passage shows its source, checksum and translation licence status.
- Voice: ask by voice, hear the translation and summary read aloud.
- Works offline.
- Free core experience. Optional study tools and voluntary donations support the project.

Important: this app is an AI-assisted search tool. It is not a religious authority, does not issue fatwas or rulings, and does not provide authoritative interpretation. Please consult qualified scholars where interpretation is required.
