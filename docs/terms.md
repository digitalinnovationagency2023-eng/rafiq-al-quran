# Terms of Use — Rafiq Al-Quran

**Last updated: 21 October 2026**
Publisher: Digital Innovative Agency FZ LLC · info@rqmbooks.com · +971 9 501 0254

1. **Free core experience.** Reading, searching, the conversational verse-finding experience and the study tools
   are free. No premium tools, subscriptions or add-ons are sold in this release, and nothing in the app requires a
   payment.
2. **Nature of the service.** The app is a Quran-focused reading and search assistant for Quranic verses and
   identified translations. It is **not** a religious authority, does not issue fatwas or rulings, and does not
   provide authoritative interpretation. Please consult qualified scholars where interpretation is required.
3. **Sources.** Quranic text and translations are served from the dataset bundled with the app; each passage shows
   its source, version and checksum. Translation licence status is shown explicitly. The optional AI service is
   never a source of Quran text, translation, verse reference or interpretation. Recitation audio comes from
   Quran.com (see item 5) and is separate from the text dataset.
4. **Optional AI assistance.** AI assistance is off by default and must be configured by the app's operator with a
   third-party AI service (the current free configuration uses a Google AI Studio / Gemini key held on the server).
   Where it is enabled and switched on by the user, it is used only to write a short neutral small-talk reply,
   suggest search words for the app's own search, read text from an attached image (OCR), and write a short
   summary of verses the app has already retrieved using only those verses and their references. It may be limited,
   rate-limited or unavailable, in which case the app falls back to its local behaviour. AI requests are processed
   transiently and the app's server stores no user text or attachments. AI output is not religious content and
   must not be treated as guidance or interpretation; all Quranic text shown comes from the bundled dataset.
5. **Recitation.** Verse recitation is enabled: audio streams from Quran.com's public CDN (reciters Mishary Rashid
   Alafasy, Abdul Basit Abdus Samad — Murattal, and Muhammad Siddiq Al-Minshawi — Murattal), with the attribution
   "Source: Quran.com" shown. The publisher's recitation rights review for this source is recorded (21 October
   2026). Nothing is stored on your device by the app. Availability depends on your internet connection and on
   Quran.com's service and may change without notice. Do not download, copy, re-upload or redistribute any
   recording through or from the app; the recitations remain the property of their owners and providers.
6. **Attachments.** You may attach text files, .docx documents or (when AI assistance is enabled) images. Text and
   .docx files are processed on your device; images are sent to the app's server and forwarded to the AI provider
   only for the single request. You must have the legal right to use anything you attach, and you must not attach
   unlawful, harmful or sensitive personal data. PDFs are not supported yet.
7. **Acceptable use.** Do not misrepresent the app's output as divine communication or as a religious ruling, and
   do not use the recitation or AI features in a way that infringes rights or breaks the law.
8. **Study data.** Bookmarks, collections, personal notes, saved searches, reading plans and reading position are
   stored on your device and are not synced to an account or another device.
9. **Liability.** The app is provided "as is". To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for decisions made
   in reliance on search results or AI-assisted output, or for third-party services (including the recitation
   service) being unavailable or incorrect.
10. **Privacy.** The handling of data is described in the Privacy Policy. Contact info@rqmbooks.com for data
    requests.
