# Privacy Policy — Rafiq Al-Quran

**Last updated: 20 October 2026**
Publisher: Digital Innovative Agency FZ LLC · info@rqmbooks.com · +971 9 501 0254

## Data stored on your device
Your questions, the answers shown, bookmarks, reading history, saved notes and preferences (language, text size, voice setting) are stored locally on your device. No account is required to use the app.

## Data that may leave your device
The core experience — reading the Quran, searching and receiving answers — works offline from a dataset bundled inside the app. Data leaves the device only in these cases:

- **Feedback reports**, when you choose to send one: category, description, interface language, optional verse reference and optional contact detail. Conversation text and diagnostics are sent only if you explicitly tick the consent box.
- **Optional online services** (purchase verification if paid tools are enabled), when the app is configured with an API address.

## Voice features
Speech input uses your device's speech recognition, which may process audio on your device or with your platform provider. Spoken answers use your device's text-to-speech. The app does not record or upload audio. Voice can be disabled in the app menu.

## Payments and donations
If optional premium tools are enabled, payments are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card details. Purchase tokens are sent to our server only to confirm entitlement. Donations are voluntary and never required to read or search the Quran.

## What we never do
We do not sell data, we do not run advertising, we do not use advertising identifiers, and the app never reads RQM Books accounting, customer, invoice or payroll data.

## Children
The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Retention and deletion
Locally stored data is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Feedback reports are kept for review and can be deleted on request. In-app conversations can be deleted from the app.

## Your choices
Use the app's Privacy page to review this information, disable voice, and delete conversations. Contact info@rqmbooks.com for data requests.

## Changes
We will update this page when the app changes; the date above shows the latest revision.
